About Me

I am currently studying a Professional Bachelor in Software Development at EK (Erhvervsakademi København), after finishing my degree as a Datamatiker at Cphbusiness Lyngby in 2026. My educational journey has given me hands-on experience with both front-end and back-end development, working with technologies such as Java, JavaScript, MySQL, pgAdmin, Spring Boot, Maven, Docker, and PostgreSQL.
